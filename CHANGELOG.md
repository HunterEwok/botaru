# Changelog

Skeleton versions: v0.1, v0.2, v0.3, v0.4. A skeleton version states which rules the conformance suite checks from that version on; what goes into each version is stated in the section "What is in which version" of every document.

## Unreleased

- Owner decision D-114, the simplification of v0.4 begins: the goals and constraints of the farm in `docs/goals.md` (G1 to G6, C1 to C10, the five critical incident classes); every rule names the goal or constraint it serves in the new required field `goal`; 41 rules not needed before the pilot are deferred with a reason and a condition of return (`conformance/deferred.md`), their mechanisms are still in the schemas and are removed later; 14 rules merged into the rule with the same behaviour (each stays as a retired row in `conformance/retired.md` that names its target), and the closed registries whose entries named them got a new version.
- Checks for the simplification of v0.4: the skeleton version v0.4; the rule status `deferred` (the full row stays with the date, the reason, the condition of return and whether the mechanism was removed; no version checks it; while its mechanism is still in the skeleton, fixtures, cases, suite checks and texts may name it (a note), and once `mechanism_removed: true` nothing may name it outside the decision log and the changelog; generated list `conformance/deferred.md`); a rule may end without a replacement (`until`, empty `superseded_by`, `end_reason`); before the first release a removed schema, closed registry or registry value, or an incompatible schema change, is accepted when recorded in `spec/removed.yaml`; the check method `impl`: a Go test of the reference implementation (`impl:<folder>/<TestName>`) covers a rule in readiness.
- Owner decisions D-109 to D-113, the rules of v0.3: the commission answers for its outcomes (forecasts scored by code, accuracy, a reward for the most accurate member, links of incidents to rejected proposals confirmed by a person, parking of a member who answers for a link, who leaves by accuracy), first in shadow and switched on by data; the farm manager is no longer stopped by an incident it could have prevented, but pages people and is restricted, and gets takeover on its model, notices from closed fields, an analysis after each incident and a monthly line; new personas with five anchors for the manager and accuracy paragraphs for the reporter and the translator; links of incidents to script runs, tenant check cases and a translator pause; resubmission and replay of rejected proposals and farm guards in every trial. 40 new rules; 13 rules of v0.2 keep their rows with `until: v0.2` and name their v0.3 replacements in `superseded_by` (D-113). New schemas `review-result.v2`, `attribution.v2`, the tools `link_hint` and `attention_notice`, the P1 operation `card.feedback`, the registry `spec/model/manager-attention.yaml`, 11 new card and notice kinds and 38 suite checks in `docs/quality.md`.
- Owner decision D-94: five rules of v0.2 that change what the core writes for every task move to v0.1: the run fingerprint (PRV-1), artifact provenance (PRV-2), a reserve and a unit for every consumable meter (BUD-5, the meter registry is now version 2), one trace per task tree and external tracing context that decides nothing (OBS-2, OBS-3). Five new suite checks in `docs/quality.md`; the `trace` field of the task record and `traceparent` of the message envelope are v0.1 fields.
- Owner decisions D-91 to D-93: the reference implementation of the core is written in Go, lives in this repository under `impl/` under the AGPL, and runs its own agent loop over P4 without a ready-made agent. The Go module skeleton `impl/` with the workflow `.github/workflows/go.yml` (gofmt, go vet, staticcheck, tests with the race detector, builds for linux/amd64 and linux/arm64, govulncheck) and Dependabot for `gomod`. The additive-only check now also rejects any constraint on a schema that was `true` or empty, `unevaluatedItems: false`, `prefixItems` where there were none, a changed `if` or `not`, and a new or changed `oneOf` branch (matched by content) that is not provably disjoint from the others.
- Owner decisions D-88 and D-89: a farm on one server and without a monitoring system (the conformance profile `monitor` for the E5 export, alarms to people as the code text `alarm_notice` without it, the watchdog `kind: watch`, backups encrypted to external storage, the example `examples/deploy/single.yaml`, the lints `watch_missing` and `backup_external_required`, the rules MON-12 and MON-13); the Contributor License Agreement `CLA.md` (draft), external pull requests after signing, signature check by a CLA bot.
- Decisions of the fourth review of the third layer (D-87): call usage in micro-euros `eur_micros` and one daily reconciliation with the provider; the transfer basis `Tenant.spec.model_transfer` instead of `transfer_basis` of the profile; the P2 secret of a channel held by the driver (`p2_token` in the `worker.enroll` response), a personal chat needs no binding; the P4 allowlist with `temperature`, `top_p`, `top_k` and a dropped `metadata`; the lease token signature is optional; checking your own bot and channel from your own folder (`--seed`, `--driver-schemas`, skipping files that are not manifests); `hello-001` on a separate person `dave`, `origin.chat` as an object in `examples/hello-bot/sequence.yaml`; the section "Before opening the repository" in `ci/README.md`.
- Decisions of the third review of the third layer (D-86): a Telegram button with `callback_data` up to 64 bytes; `worker_id` and the core signature in the lease token, the refusal `lease_mismatch`; `Botaru-On-Behalf` only from the driver of a person's channel; the condition of the test-control port (the test bench key, a store flag, no tenant other than `kind: test` over the whole life of the store); P4 is not a transparent proxy, JSON on inputs follows I-JSON; `transfer_basis` on the model profile; the audit head with `epoch` and `key_id`; erasure with `needles`; rounding usage up to the euro cent and a daily reconciliation; hello-bot follows the fixture envelope with `ai_notice` and `privacy_notice`; three readiness numbers; your own additions are checked from your own folder; an ADR for a change of a port or a contract; training examples without buoys, sequencing and protected areas.
- Decisions of the second review of the third layer (D-85): a principal in core records is the opaque id `human:p_<ULID>`; the reference binding of P4 to the Anthropic Messages API; the audit head signature Ed25519 and SHA-256 hashes over JSON in RFC 8785 form (`spec/hashing.md`); the test bench control port `spec/ports/test-control.md`; suites stay descriptions of checks; README and the short part of CONTRIBUTING in English, the "English" column in the glossary; the default time zone `UTC`, the training time zone of examples `Europe/Berlin`.
- CONTRIBUTING: the table "what I add, where I put it, what I run" with the `ci/` commands, the fields of a rule row, the contribution terms (external pull requests after the owner publishes the terms, issues until then). The folder `docs/adr/` with the MADR 4.0.0 template.
- The third layer of v0.1: JSON Schema files in `spec/model/` and `spec/manifests/`, closed registries, the generated lifecycles `spec/lifecycles/*.yaml`, the fixtures `conformance/fixtures/`, the suites `conformance/suites/`, eval cases and examples on the tenants `demo` and `demo2`.
- The `ci/` checks in Python and the workflow `.github/workflows/skeleton.yml`: the rule registry, file generation, text and links, schemas, manifests, evals, additive-only schema changes, public safety and version readiness. All 380 rules of v0.1 have a described check: data that the skeleton CI runs, data for an implementation's runner or eval, or a description of a suite check; readiness shows these three numbers separately (D-85 (5), D-86 (11)).
- The license GNU AGPL-3.0-only (D-83) instead of Apache 2.0.
