# Changelog

Skeleton versions: v0.1, v0.2, v0.3, v0.4. A skeleton version states which rules the conformance suite checks from that version on; what goes into each version is stated in the section "What is in which version" of every document.

## Unreleased

- impl: service control without a card for a restart from the catalog's `no_card` under a `service_grant`, by card for start, stop and after untrusted text, with the initiator's own right, holds, the hourly limits with `alarm.service.control.rate` and one operation per service at a time (MON-8, APR-18, INJ-15); the commands `/service` and `/run` with routine pipelines (PRM-18, APR-17); the plugins `service` (systemctl) and `queue` (the oneshot queue) and `botaru-plugin polkit`, which prints the polkit rule of a machine from the catalog (DEP-4).
- The card schema gets the optional fields `state` (the state of the subject when the card was drawn, for an update in place) and `reminder` (a reminder card); the code text catalog gets the `card.*` texts of an action approval card, its buttons, its answers and its "Details" view.
- impl: actions by manifest with approval cards: the built-in tool `request_action`, approvals, cards drawn from the record, P1 `decide`, `card.details` and `card.remind`, the action task with its write call executed at most once, reminders, expiry and cancellation by holds, offboarding and revoked rights (APR-1 to APR-5, APR-7, APR-10, APR-11, APR-19, CNT-7, MAN-9); Telegram cards with inline buttons and edits in place.
- The events catalog gets `alarm.provider.config.v1`, the alarm by code of GW-13 for a provider configuration error, which had no catalog entry.
- impl: provider pairs with a circuit breaker, the wait for a provider rate limit and the wait for a configuration fix, seen by the dispatcher (GW-10, GW-11, GW-13, CAP-2); the running ceiling of dispatch rules (DSP-7); the 80% threshold event and the waiting reserve exception (BUD-4).
- The P1 `hello` and `heartbeat` request schemas get the optional field `health`, a list of `health-report` records, so a toolhost can report the state of each plugin as OBS-10 requires; the rule had no field to carry the report.
- impl: P2 `status`, `doctor`, `deadletter.list`, `task.redrive`, `delivery.redrive` and `audit.query` (DLV-4, AUD-7, MAN-8), and plugin health from toolhost reports (OBS-10).
- impl: P2 `health` reports the tick age of each background loop from the store, the age of the oldest urgent task, the share of deliveries delivered within 10 minutes and the free space of the store volume; the new `health` loop raises their alarms, the soft threshold of the store volume refuses enqueues of classes 3-5 and the hard one answers `not_ready` on P1, and P1 answers `not_ready` while the store is unavailable (OBS-4, OBS-8, OBS-9, CAP-5).
- The activity schema gets the optional fields `forwarded_from` and `quoted_from`: the identity of the author of forwarded or quoted text when the messenger shows a person, so the core can record that person in `personal_subjects` (AUD-5). The Telegram binding fills them from `forward_origin`, the replied message of a `quote` and `external_reply`.
- The code text catalog gets `delivery.withheld.channel_ceiling`, the text a person gets in place of an answer above the ceiling of the channel (SEC-9).
- The code text catalog gets `notice.alarm`, the text of the `alarm_notice` delivery to admins in an installation without the monitoring system (MON-13).
- Owner decision D-117, commission lenses: every member of the commission represents one lens with its own persona addendum in the tenant charter (`commission.lenses.<lens>.persona`), and the independence of the lenses rests on these personas and blind reviews, not on model families: the rule on different model families is retired and the reason `veto_lenses_same_family` is kept only as a retired entry. The skeleton adds the lenses `speed` and `clarity` without a veto (lenses registry version 3), and a tenant adds its own lenses `x_<name>` without a veto, with a meaning and a persona addendum, reviewed by the type `review.lens.v1` (the new rule COM-19, the apply reasons `lens_persona_missing` and `lens_unknown`). Example addenda are in `examples/personas/`.
- Contracts only at the boundaries (D-114, C10): contracts with checks stay only at P1, E1, P3 with E3, P4 and E5; the pages of P2, C1-C4, E2, E4, the test-control port, the consent adapter and the background loops become short descriptions of how the core is built, and `spec/ports/README.md` separates the two. The operation map is version 2: `contract: false` marks the rows of those ports (P2 schemas are implementation schemas of the core and `farmctl`), and `operator_only` on P2 rows holds the closed list of PRM-14. Fourteen schemas nothing checked are removed (the E4 describe answer, run request, run outcome and cancel request; five test-control schemas; five C1 record schemas), and the permission table and stand step schemas stay as core test schemas. The agent worker declares its runtime in the new `runtime` field of P1 `hello`, and the attempt work item carries `reply_locale` and `skills`. RT-1, RT-2 and RT-5 become core rules about what the core requires of any runtime. The suites `store`, `runtime` and `sink` are gone: their checks move to `core-blackbox` (and `model-gateway`, `toolhost`), AUD-3 and FEN-2 are also covered by Go tests of the reference, and the check method `store` is removed.
- Owner decisions D-115 and D-116, the commission of v0.4 (D-114): the secret mandatory vote "who leaves" is replaced, code proposes the leaver by accuracy (GOV-43) and otherwise a person chooses it from the list of seats on the promotion card with the figures of every seat and "Not now", and only a person's button makes it effective; ballots, their package and reasons, case rounds, vote counting, reissues, the flag "stuck" and the card `governance_stuck`, `wrong_leaver` and the flags of the vote are removed (five rules of the vote, its count, its money and its stuck rounds and the single-model flag retired; see `conformance/retired.md` and `spec/removed.yaml`); one accuracy measure per lens, 1 - B / B_ref, with the calibration rule merged into it (COM-17); the threshold of scored forecasts for switching accountability on is the charter field `commission.accountability_min_forecasts` (20 by default, never below 8, D-116), and forecasts are scored from the first `apply` on v0.3; the audit sample is a uniform draw by a recorded seed (DIG-5); one running trial per field of an object (PRP-9); the farm manager is an advisory lens that takes no part in choosing who leaves (GOV-25); the necessity lens sees only borderline escalations (COM-13).
- Simpler money and less work beside the monitoring system (D-114): money is the one reserved unit (BUD-5, four meter values removed, a paid tool declares `billing: external` with `price_eur`); code reconciles an imported provider report with a release by time of unreconciled estimates and an overdue threshold set to the reconciliation rhythm (BUD-10); one formula for review money (BUD-13), actual usage per line in `doctor` instead of the price table (BUD-15), the workspace check only at `apply` without a daily probe (GW-9, the loop `provider_check` is gone), a provider rate limit as a wait without a limiter of our own (GW-10); the coverage lint of the manager line merged into MGR-49; volume quotas belong to the file system and the monitoring system (CAP-6), the core raises backup alarms only without the monitoring system (RET-8), and the per-tenant `doctor` lines are the alarm path (OBS-11).
- Simplification of v0.4 (D-114), reports to people and scripts: a model paragraph to people carries no numbers at all, only names and the digest's own dates (REP-4, new fixture `fx-rep-no-numbers`); a newer digest over the pace ceiling replaces the waiting one (REP-10); the schedule check before "Accept" keeps only validity and the disappearing and repeating local times (TIM-5); a script condition keeps `eq`, `ne`, `in` over closed fields and step states, while rollups and `items.from` selections are deferred (SCR-3, new deferred SCR-15, `condition-ops` version 2); the translator pause becomes the auto-hold of any role after three confirmed incident links (the new HLD-9, which replaces the translator's own pause); a person's copy of their data goes only to them, and the separate `/mydata` report and `principal.data_report` are dropped (SEC-10).
- D-114, the farm manager and the bot's memory and identity simplified: the manager has two limits (`ops_per_hour` and `hold_ttl_max`) and the safeguard after refusals in a row, without a separate pause of its money (the operation `P2.manager.resume`, `paused_in` and the notice `manager_spend_paused` are removed); the tenant's relation to the manager is the one flag `farm_manager.enabled`; escalation rates per worker and per tenant only; "preventable" (MGR-45, v0.3) is measured by code on the free-space series and covers each critical incident class of `docs/goals.md`, stating which ones the manager cannot prevent; facts pass to the successor by code, rechecked and marked inherited, without a choice task, a person or the `handover` purpose; the bot persona lint checks the file only; the personal assistant (DSP-10) is deferred, and from v0.2 `apply` rejects a role with `memory: person` (`person_memory_deferred`). Removed schemas, fields and registry values are listed in `spec/removed.yaml`.
- Owner decision D-114, the simplification of v0.4 begins: the goals and constraints of the farm in `docs/goals.md` (G1 to G6, C1 to C10, the five critical incident classes); every rule names the goal or constraint it serves in the new required field `goal`; 41 rules not needed before the pilot are deferred with a reason and a condition of return (`conformance/deferred.md`), their mechanisms are still in the schemas and are removed later; 14 rules merged into the rule with the same behaviour (each stays as a retired row in `conformance/retired.md` that names its target), and the closed registries whose entries named them got a new version.
